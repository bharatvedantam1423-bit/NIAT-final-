# Variation B: intake, section map and refined copy

## Intake
- **Product:** NIAT. Industry-ready upskilling in AI/ML, robotics and data science, delivered alongside a UGC-recognised B.Tech at 35+ partner universities. An initiative by NxtWave; NIAT is not a university.
- **Target users:** Class 12 students (17–19) choosing engineering, who want skills and a career edge. Their parents, who decide and pay, want a valid degree, safety and honest outcomes.
- **Primary goal:** Apply for the NAT (NxtWave Admission-counselling Test).
- **Secondary goals:** Download the brochure; talk to a counsellor on WhatsApp.
- **Platform:** both, designed mobile-first. Most traffic is Android phones.
- **Tone:** confident, specific, honest. Aspirational for students, reassuring for parents. No hype words.
- **Must-haves:** outcome disclaimers, the IIT Kharagpur OCN disclaimer, "NIAT is not a university", and the existing live links.

## Compliance changes (from the research)
Sources: ASCI education guidelines (rev. May 2023), CCPA Coaching Guidelines (Nov 2024), CCPA Dark Patterns Guidelines (2023), UGC public notices. This is research, not legal advice; legal should review final claim wording.
- **Same-size disclaimers:** outcome disclaimers sit next to the claims at the same type size, including "Past record is no guarantee of future prospects".
- **No "highest package" headline:** ASCI wants package claims tied to the latest completed academic year with the batch size. Until the team can supply year + batch size, Variation B shows individual offers with names (with consent) rather than a "highest package" stat.
- **University wording:** "UGC-recognised partner universities; degree awarded by [University]". Never imply UGC endorses NIAT.
- **No false urgency:** no countdowns, "seats left" or "slots booked in the last 2 hours".
- **Testimonials:** use only stories with written consent taken after selection (CCPA 2024).
- **Lead forms and minors:** DPDP Rules require verifiable parental consent for under-18s once in force (about May 2027). The NAT application flow will need a parent-consent step.
