# Reference report: NIAT home page, Variation B

Searched 11 sections · 63 references reviewed on Mobbin.
References are for learning patterns only. The wireframe uses NIAT's own copy and structure.

## 01 · Hero
**Common patterns:** one headline and one primary CTA. Either centred over full-bleed photography, or split with a supporting panel beside it.
**Best references:**
- [Runway, Creative Partners hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/ad0547f0-4f1b-427b-8c57-34484ba6ae63): centred serif headline over a monochrome full-bleed photo, a single small "Apply Now" button.
- [Webflow Community hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/2c4b062a-58f3-49ab-9443-8d93d1389e9e): centred question headline framed by a collage of real people, one button.
- [Brilliant, educator hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/f3595c98-f273-4c51-bc46-9e0228acb5c9): split. Pitch and CTA on the left, "How to get started" numbered steps on the right.
**Borrow:** a single primary CTA (Brilliant, Runway). Brilliant's idea of answering "what happens next" right next to the CTA.
**Avoid:** small CTAs on dark photos (Runway's button is easy to miss on mobile).
**Direction:** A · Split hero with "next steps" panel (recommended) · B · Centred hero with student collage.
**Laws seen:** Hick's (one CTA), Goal-Gradient (steps beside CTA), Aesthetic-Usability.

## 02 · Key stats
**Common patterns:** 3–4 numbers in a row, short labels, hairline dividers. Footnote markers on claims.
**Best references:**
- [Slack, "Investing in teamwork pays off"](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/53c33050-78c8-43d5-ba24-92277cedeaa4): 4 stats, each with an asterisk tied to a footnote.
- [Amigo stats band](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/1a3250eb-e77f-4fb9-aa57-12eec3869d08): large serif numerals, labels above, vertical dividers.
- [Base metrics row](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/7cd597cc-53a4-42a0-b1e1-f558324c22f1): mono uppercase labels under numbers.
**Borrow:** footnote markers on every outcome claim (Slack). Needed for NIAT's disclaimers.
**Avoid:** bento stat walls with empty tiles (Robot.com) that look padded.
**Direction:** single 4-up row with footnotes. No variation.
**Laws seen:** Chunking, Cognitive Bias (anchoring), Von Restorff when one stat is set larger.

## 03 · How NIAT works (degree + skills explainer)
**Common patterns:** 3 cards ("join → do → earn"), or a hub-and-spoke diagram.
**Best references:**
- [Notion, How it works](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/33356b41-681b-4723-93f8-ac7c49022977): 3 equal cards with title, one paragraph and a simple illustration.
- [Coda, What are Packs](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/a160ad8c-9830-4919-81f4-af8d388603d0): text on the left, hub-and-spoke diagram on the right.
- [Intercom, Built to work together](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/dadb993c-7333-49fe-a338-95b023dd2a5d): a diagram of two systems working as one, with 3 explanatory cards beside it.
**Borrow:** a simple "University + NIAT = You" diagram (Intercom/Coda) to settle the most common confusion.
**Avoid:** abstract diagrams with no labels.
**Direction:** equation diagram plus 3 short cards. No variation.
**Laws seen:** Mental Model, Uniform Connectedness, Prägnanz.

## 04 · Curriculum journey (4 years)
**Common patterns:** horizontal timeline with dots, vertical alternating timeline, big-year scrubber.
**Best references:**
- [Slash, Our story](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/2acef3f3-6363-43c3-b1f8-9c457b424f27): vertical timeline, year headings alternating with photos.
- [AngelList, Our evolution](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/33919649-f1f9-4bf2-becc-f74a1c453876): huge year numerals as a scrubber, the active one highlighted, detail below.
- [Grok, Path of progress](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/5809b85f-2e44-4d26-998f-9fb4e2379f13): minimal horizontal line with dated dots.
**Borrow:** a visible line connecting the 4 years (Uniform Connectedness). AngelList's big-numeral selector as the tab control.
**Avoid:** horizontal-only timelines that need sideways scrolling on mobile.
**Direction:** A · Vertical stepped timeline (recommended, mobile-first) · B · Big-numeral year selector with a detail panel.
**Laws seen:** Uniform Connectedness, Goal-Gradient, Serial Position.

## 05 · Benefits (IIT KGP OCN, ₹30,000/project, ₹100 Cr tokens)
**Common patterns:** 3–5 equal cards, or tinted stat cards with a one-line "for…" explanation.
**Best references:**
- [Amplemarket, Why users prefer](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/d0b5d296-0f9b-4dd2-8ab4-af4fb28928e1): 5 tinted cards, big number then label then one-line reason.
- [Fluz metrics](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/2cd277b3-579b-4d9d-8a70-bc882293c9e7): number, then a 2-line plain-language explanation.
**Borrow:** number → label → plain reason → fine print. Every card has the same anatomy.
**Avoid:** icon-only benefit grids (Retool) that read as generic perks.
**Direction:** 3 cards, with the IIT KGP card set wider. No variation.
**Laws seen:** Von Restorff, Similarity, Chunking.

## 06 · Student outcomes (placements, wins, startups)
**Common patterns:** quote cards with avatar, tall photo cards with tags, case-study cards.
**Best references:**
- [Kajabi creator cards](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/05a0a50b-1ac3-4b9c-a92a-0af404cd894e): tall photo, name, 2 category tags, short quote, "Read more".
- [monday U, students fast-tracked](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/8fc0d3ed-d506-465c-a2f3-f1443a81ae00): quote, avatar, name and programme.
- [Dovetail customers](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/17322e8a-ddc6-4d37-a16c-c0382552e02c): photo, title, description and 3 metadata rows.
**Borrow:** tags on each story (Placement / Startup / Hackathon) so one rail can hold all proof types. Metadata rows for role, company and package.
**Avoid:** quote-only cards with no outcome. They don't answer "did it work?".
**Direction:** A · Filterable story rail (Placements / Startups / Wins) (recommended) · B · Separate blocks per proof type, as in Variation A.
**Laws seen:** Cognitive Bias (social proof), Chunking, Choice Overload (the filter limits the set).

## 07 · Mentors
**Common patterns:** photo grid with name and title. Strong versions add "Past: …" credibility lines.
**Best references:**
- [Linear team](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/82c5e72f-ad2a-4d51-9b42-cd220dedfffc): monochrome photos, name, role, "Past: Airbnb, Coinbase".
- [Descript team](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/3bfabec1-1ba0-44be-a38a-8eb38416338c): horizontal cards with photo, name and role.
- [Height team](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/c317bfca-01f4-4e4a-ade4-0109d694b919): dense 3-column list with small photos.
**Borrow:** Linear's role-plus-company line, and consistent photo treatment.
**Avoid:** 12 equal large tiles. Height's denser list keeps the page short.
**Direction:** compact list grid (photo, name, role · company). No variation.
**Laws seen:** Similarity, Proximity, Aesthetic-Usability.

## 08 · Campuses
**Common patterns:** region tabs with a city list, photo cards per location, big typographic list.
**Best references:**
- [Slack, Explore by region](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/cdf87d42-8bd8-4238-b4a9-8e90abfc1ebc): region tabs, then a 4-column list of cities.
- [Udemy locations](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/1074654d-bdc6-4fb5-baf0-11afc1cdcd60): 2 featured photo cards plus a row of small city cards.
- [Craft Agency locations](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/b9f10246-c8f9-47a1-9b5b-777dd981534e): oversized typographic city list.
**Borrow:** a state filter (Slack) so a student finds "near me" fast, and featured photo cards (Udemy).
**Avoid:** a flat list of 35 names with no grouping.
**Direction:** state chips that filter campus cards. No variation.
**Laws seen:** Hick's, Choice Overload, Jakob's (tabs and filters).

## 09 · Admissions steps
**Common patterns:** 3–4 numbered cards plus a CTA, or a numbered accordion beside a headline and CTA.
**Best references:**
- [OpenPhone, Ready to get started](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/785395c6-a1e0-4b50-8c20-d07a15aef374): 3 large numbered cards with a CTA centred below.
- [Apollo, Ready to partner up](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/d1dd71e8-d5d9-467b-b577-cd2efadbdb27): headline and CTA left, numbered accordion right.
- [Sketch, application process](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/7ab14130-50c7-490f-8a05-262e5e448b19): reassuring copy left, numbered accordion right.
**Borrow:** Apollo/Sketch split. The CTA stays in view while details expand on demand (progressive disclosure).
**Avoid:** steps with no CTA attached.
**Direction:** split. Headline, reassurance and CTA on the left; a 4-step connected list on the right. No variation.
**Laws seen:** Goal-Gradient, Uniform Connectedness, Fitts's.

## 10 · FAQ
**Common patterns:** grouped accordions with category labels in a left column.
**Best references:**
- [YLLW FAQ](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/5a01248f-1cb0-4b98-af99-5722b7c05f27): category name left, accordions right, one group per row.
- [Zendesk FAQ](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/2c641d30-da0d-43d7-8dc4-03ebe8967397): small category labels above grouped accordions.
- [Klarna FAQ](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/47cd2bd7-004b-4789-8f21-4269e3dfc29a): search plus two-column category accordions.
**Borrow:** grouping by audience question (About NIAT / Degree / NAT / Campus).
**Avoid:** search on a short FAQ (over-built).
**Direction:** category tabs over one accordion list. No variation.
**Laws seen:** Chunking, Progressive disclosure, Miller's.

## 11 · Final CTA
**Common patterns:** short headline, one line of reassurance, 1–2 buttons, calm background.
**Best references:**
- [Midday closing CTA](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/b8c8a83a-34c5-48e4-8509-e3b028aac152): headline, reassurance line, primary plus secondary button.
- [Dropbox closing band](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/04aa390b-5421-43a5-8f43-950bc6c9886f): headline left, single CTA right.
**Borrow:** reassurance microcopy directly under the headline.
**Avoid:** gimmicky copy ("Still scrolling?") for a parent audience.
**Direction:** centred, primary plus secondary. No variation.
**Laws seen:** Peak-End, Serial Position.
