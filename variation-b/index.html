What is NIAT?

NIAT (NxtWave of Innovation in Advanced Technologies) is an upskilling initiative by NxtWave that bridges the gap between industry and academia. Its industry-aligned upskilling programme is offered at 35+ universities across India.

What makes the programme different?
  • Deep industry integration: insights from 3,000+ companies, 10,000+ tech professionals and in-house R&D that universities use to modernise their curriculum.
  • Practitioner-led faculty support: industry-certified faculty upskilling, case-based labs and Professors of Practice who co-deliver and mentor.
  • AI-enabled experiential learning: project-based learning, live assessments and real-time feedback.
  • Global-standard infrastructure: a research-backed classroom design playbook tailored for Indian campuses.
Where is the programme offered?

At 35+ UGC-recognised partner universities across 11 states. The NAT and a counselling session help you find the right one.

Is the B.Tech degree awarded by NIAT?

No. You receive a UGC-recognised / AICTE-approved B.Tech from the university you enrol in, after completing its coursework, exams and credits as per UGC and AICTE guidelines. NIAT awards an Industry Ready Certificate; its upskilling is non-credit and does not affect degree progression.

Is the degree valid for jobs and higher studies abroad?

Yes. The B.Tech is delivered and awarded by UGC-recognised / AICTE-approved universities, so it is valid for all government and private jobs and for higher studies in India and abroad.

What are the eligibility criteria for admission?

NIAT runs assessments and counselling to understand your skills, interests and aptitude, then recommends suitable universities. To be admitted you must meet the university's eligibility criteria and admission policies; admission is solely at the university's discretion.

What is the NAT and is it mandatory?

The NxtWave Admission-counselling Test (NAT) is a 90-minute online test of problem-solving, critical thinking and mathematics. It is mandatory for admission counselling from NIAT.

What is the NAT exam pattern?
  • Psychometric test: 44 questions · 25 min · 44 marks. No right or wrong answers.
  • Critical thinking & learnability: 20 questions · 30 min · 20 marks.
  • Mathematics: 18 questions · 35 min · 36 marks.

There is no negative marking.

When can I take the NAT?

The NAT runs multiple times a month. Pick the nearest available slot when you apply.

How should I prepare?

Solve reasoning problems and puzzles daily, practise JEE / CET / Olympiad-style questions, read comprehension passages, and revise core maths concepts for understanding rather than memorisation.
